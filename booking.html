Help and booking guidance

Use this page to understand how to browse doctor schedules, jump to a specific date, and create or cancel reservations directly from the booking grid. The help content stays inside the same booking page so you can switch back to bookings without losing context.

How to use booking

Select a doctor from the dropdown, review the weekly timetable, and click on an available slot to open the reservation details. Use Jump To Date to move the calendar to another week, and visit My Bookings to review or cancel existing reservations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?
Choose a doctor, select an available time slot in the table, enter a short summary, and submit the reservation.
How do I jump to another week?
Use the Jump To Date field near the top of the page and enter a date in DD/MM/YYYY format. The schedule will move to the week containing that date.
Where can I cancel a reservation?
Open My Bookings, click the reservation card, and use the cancel option in the details dialog.
What if a slot looks unavailable?
Unavailable slots are outside the doctor’s working hours or already reserved. Only available slots can be booked.
